# LineFlow AI

LineFlow AI is a material ordering, delivery, inventory, and production-flow system for four assembly lines.

## Live Demo

**https://lineflow-ai.vercel.app**

The demo is publicly shareable and automatically reflects production deployments from the connected project.

## How It Works

1. Assembly-line users log in and order material.
2. Orders automatically route to the responsible material handler.
3. Handlers accept, pick up, transport, and deliver material.
4. LineFlow tracks delivery time, inventory quantity, and material location.
5. Leadership monitors production plans, inventory, goals, alerts, and performance.

## Delivery Workflows

- **Hoods → Tristen → Forklift**
- **Booms → Greg → Combi Lift**
- Additional materials and backup handlers can be assigned as needed.

## Users & Access

- **Lines 1–4** — place orders and track their requests
- **Greg** — Boom Material Handler / Combi Lift workflow
- **Tristen** — Hood Material Handler / Forklift workflow
- **Debbie** — Planner / System Administrator
- **Tammy** — Supervisor / System Administrator
- **Chance** — Supervisor / System Administrator

Debbie, Tammy, and Chance have full operational and administrative access. All important changes are timestamped and saved in the audit history.

## Daily Production Plan

Before each shift, leadership can publish the day's plan showing booms/material scheduled to:

- Start
- Continue
- Complete
- Hold
- Wait on Material

Plans can be changed during the shift. Changes record who made them, when, why, and which users are affected.

## Inventory & Alerts

- Live quantity on hand by location
- Material in transit and at assembly lines
- Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts
- Shortage and overage alerts
- Damaged, missing, returned, or incorrect material
- Inventory discrepancies and manual adjustments
- Material readiness checks against the daily plan
- Reorder/safety-stock levels
- Full inventory movement history

## Goals & Performance

Leadership can create and track **weekly and monthly goals**, including delivery time, on-time delivery rate, inventory accuracy, shortages, stockouts, damaged material, and production targets.

Greg and Tristen receive an **end-of-shift performance summary** with deliveries completed, average delivery time, on-time rate, issues reported, and other relevant shift metrics. Users can also view Today / Week / Month performance trends.

## Supervisor / Planner Control Center

Debbie, Tammy, and Chance can view and manage:

- All four assembly lines
- Daily production plan
- Live material requests and deliveries
- Greg and Tristen workflows
- Inventory and material locations
- Low-stock, damage, shortage, and overage alerts
- Weekly and monthly goals
- Material readiness
- Workflow and priority changes
- Daily/weekly/monthly reports
- Shift handoff notes
- Open operational issues
- Audit history

## Core Features

- Separate logins and role-based dashboards
- Automatic request routing
- Request-to-delivery timestamps
- Live inventory and location tracking
- 31 hood rows + 1 miscellaneous row
- Boom material locations and part numbers
- Delivery history and reporting
- Start-of-shift production plan
- End-of-shift worker summaries
